Project status

Prototype

The board is fully routed (v0.11 working revision; v0.10 frozen 2026-11-16), and the robot body is designed and interference-checked in CAD. Neither has been built and tested yet. Values in these guides come from the design files. Where a measurement on your hardware disagrees, trust the measurement and tell us.
