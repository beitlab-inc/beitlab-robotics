Recommended path

If this is your first robot from us, work through the guides in this order. Each one assumes the previous one passed.

  1. Connect the Duo S to your PCSet up the development computer, flash the Milk-V Duo S and log in over USB before it goes anywhere near the carrier. First steps →
  2. Bring up the Robotics BoardInspect the board, then power it on stage by stage on a current-limited supply: rails, safety chain, Duo S, sensors, one motor at a time. Bring-up →
  3. Assemble the robotPrint the parts, fit the servos and legs, mount the electronics and wire the harnesses. Assembly guide →
  4. Calibrate, then balanceCommission the motors and knees against their sensors, measure the mass properties and design the gains. The technical book covers commissioning and tuning in depth.

Project status

Prototype

The board is fully routed and frozen (v0.8, 2026-11-11), and the robot body is designed and interference-checked in CAD. Neither has been built and tested yet. Values in these guides come from the design files. Where a measurement on your hardware disagrees, trust the measurement and tell us.
