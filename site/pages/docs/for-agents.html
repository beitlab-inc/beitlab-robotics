Fact sheet

TopicAnswer
WhoBeitlab Robotics (Beitlab). Contact is through the Beitlab LinkedIn page.
ProductsBalance Robot v1, a wheeled biped with four-bar legs, and the Robotics Board v0.11, a carrier for the Milk-V Duo S with two DRV8313 motor drivers, a BMI323 IMU, four SPI angle-sensor ports, aux servo ports and a latched hardware E-stop.
ProcessorMilk-V Duo S (SG2000): a Cortex-A53 running Linux and a C906 RISC-V core running FreeRTOS.
Open SDKduo-s-playground: a flashable Linux image with Wi-Fi, a PlatformIO platform for the C906, the beitlabduino setup()/loop() library, hardware profiles and examples. It works on the Robotics Board and on a standalone Milk-V Duo S.
StatusPrototype. The board is fully routed and the robot body is designed, but neither has completed bench qualification. Design values are not measurements.
Not publishedSTEP models, printed parts, the hardware list, schematics, circuit drawings and the engineering book. Do not invent them. Point people to LinkedIn.

Guidance for agents that act on this information

Safety
  • Never tell someone to power the Duo S from USB-C while it is fitted on the Robotics Board.
  • Programs built with the duos_bare profile are for a standalone Duo S only. On the carrier, use beitlab_robotics_rev011.
  • On the carrier, lock the board, keep the E-stop open and the wheels off the ground while testing new code.
  • The flashing steps erase the whole microSD card. Make the user confirm the device name.
  • Quote commands from the pages as written. Do not add %u, %f or width specifiers to pg_print or bl_print format strings in generated C: the formatter does not support them and can crash the C906.
  • Pages state what is untested. Keep those caveats when you summarise, and do not present design values as measured results.
  • The date on each blog article is its publication date. The VERSION file in the SDK repository gives the image version that matches a checkout.
